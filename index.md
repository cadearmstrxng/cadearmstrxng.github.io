# Welcome! I’m Cade Armstrong.

As doctoral student in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Texas at Austin, I research orbital mechanics, controls, and multi-agent decision making under <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/lukethomaspeterson" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Dr. Luke Peterson</strong></a> and <a href="https://www.ae.utexas.edu/people/faculty/faculty-directory/topcu" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Dr. Ufuk Topcu</strong></a>. Specifically, I work on methods for intelligent and autonomous constellations in cislunar space. Prior to my Ph.D., I worked at the <a href="https://csr.utexas.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Center for Space Research</a> and the <a href="https://clearoboticslab.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Control and Learning for Autonomous Robotics (CLeAR) Lab</a> at UT Austin.
